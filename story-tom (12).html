Tom's story.

Tom Pollock · Partner · 13 years at Oliver Parks

TP

Tom Pollock

Partner
13 years at Oliver Parks

Why did you join Oliver Parks?

Tom has been with Oliver Parks since day one. He joined for the business vision — something different from a typical high-street recruitment agency — and the chance to build a long-term career and a business of his own, including the opportunity to work in the US market. He started as a Senior Recruiter and has risen to lead the Supply Chain business unit.

What is the best, most rewarding thing about working at OP?

For Tom, the people are the firm's greatest asset: very capable colleagues he learns from every day, a welcoming, high-performing culture, and the reward of watching people progress week to week. He also values the flexibility and the perks — longer lunch breaks for gym-goers, monthly lunch clubs, annual high-performer trips and summer and winter parties.
