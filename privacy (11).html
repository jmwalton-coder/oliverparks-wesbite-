3. The data we collect

We may collect, use, store and transfer the following kinds of personal data about you:

  • Identity data – such as your name and job title.
  • Contact data – such as your email address, phone number and address.
  • Career data – such as your CV, employment history, skills and qualifications.
  • Financial data – such as your salary history and expectations.
  • Transaction and usage data – such as the roles you are interested in, your employment status and how we have helped you before.

We do not collect special category data (for example health, religion or ethnicity) or information about criminal convictions.

4. How we collect your data

  • Directly from you – when you fill in forms, send us your CV or contact us by post, phone, email or otherwise.
  • From third parties and public sources – such as LinkedIn, XING, Companies House, the German commercial register and other publicly available sources.

5. How we use your data and our legal bases

We use your data to register you as a candidate, match you with suitable job opportunities, manage our relationship with you and with our clients, administer and protect our business and website, and recommend services that may interest you. We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – to take steps before, or to perform, a contract with you;
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – for example to contact professionals about relevant roles and to run our business, where your interests and rights do not override ours;
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) – where we must comply with the law; and
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) – generally only for marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We only use your data for the purpose we collected it for, unless a new purpose is compatible with the original one. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

6. Sharing your data

We may share your personal data with other Oliver Parks group companies in the UK, Ireland, Germany and the US; with trusted external third parties, such as clients considering you for a role and service providers who support our business (for example IT, hosting and recruitment software providers); and with any party that acquires or merges with our business.

We require all third parties to respect the security of your data and to treat it in line with the law. Service providers may only process your data on our instructions and under a data processing agreement.

7. International transfers

Because the Oliver Parks group includes companies in the UK, the EU and the US, your personal data may be transferred between countries — for example when a consultant in another office works on a role with you.

The UK and the EU recognise each other as providing adequate protection. Where we transfer data to a country without such recognition, such as the US, we use appropriate safeguards — for example the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum) or certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards.

8. Cookies and our website

When you visit our website, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view, to deliver the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Data security

We have security measures in place to prevent your personal data from being lost, used or accessed without authorisation, altered or disclosed. Access is limited to employees and partners who have a business need to know.

We have procedures to deal with any suspected data breach and will notify you and the relevant regulator where we are legally required to.

10. How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes we collected it for, including any legal, tax, accounting or reporting requirements. In most cases this is eight years from our last contact with you.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 11). We may keep anonymised information, which can no longer identify you, indefinitely.

11. Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • request correction of your personal data (Art. 16);
  • request erasure of your personal data (Art. 17);
  • request restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • request transfer of your personal data (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time (Art. 21); and
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before you withdrew it.

These rights also apply to data held by our service providers. To exercise any of them, email info@oliverparks.com. There is usually no fee, and we aim to respond within one month — longer only if your request is particularly complex or you have made several requests.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work. The authorities for our European companies are:

  • UK (Oliver Parks Ltd): Information Commissioner's Office – ico.org.uk
  • Ireland (Oliver Parks (Europe) Ltd): Data Protection Commission – dataprotection.ie
  • Germany (Oliver Parks GmbH): Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen – ldi.nrw.de

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
